the case text and read access to the repository skills only. No conversation, rubric or earlier
output was passed. "Before" runs used an isolated worktree at the commit before the change; "after"
runs used the changed working tree. One person graded every output against the rubric, after the
run. This records observed behavior only; it is not a model comparison or a quality benchmark.

## Broad-peer framing on the `abstract-section` input

Three criteria were written down before these two runs: A1, the first sentence states a biological
stake rather than the prediction task, a technology or a dataset; A2, the method is introduced by
what it lets a biologist do before any model family; A3, findings are stated as what was predicted,
not as metric names.

| Run | A1 | A2 | A3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| After 1 | yes | yes | yes |
| After 2 | yes | yes | yes |

Opening sentences: "A cell's transcriptional response to genetic perturbation reveals what genes do
and how they interact." and "A cell's transcriptional response to genetic perturbation reveals gene
function and genetic interactions, but possible perturbations, especially gene combinations, far
outnumber those that can be tested experimentally." Both runs left a marked placeholder for the gap
sentence because no Introduction was supplied.

For comparison only: the two earlier runs on this input (before and after the section contracts)
both opened with "Predicting ... responses to ... perturbations ...", which fails A1. Those two were
graded on A1 to A3 after the criteria were written, so they are not a controlled baseline.

## Limits

Two runs per arm, one grader, synthetic cases. The comparison on the `abstract-section` input is
not controlled. A pass shows that the instructions were followed in these runs, not that the
resulting abstracts are better for readers.
