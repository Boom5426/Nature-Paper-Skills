> Choosing which perturbations to profile is ultimately not only a modelling challenge but a decision problem in its own right. Here we present DoseRank, which ranks untested drug–dose pairs using deployment-available prior knowledge. In this way, DoseRank turns prior knowledge into experimental priorities. Together, these results extend our understanding of how cells respond to perturbation.

Results 中的一段：

> What, then, drives the gain? **Together, these analyses show that the gain comes from the dose module rather than from model size**: removing the dose module lowered top-10 precision from 0.62 to 0.48, whereas doubling the number of parameters changed it by less than 0.01. The ranking was robust to the choice of k (Spearman ρ = 0.91 across k = 5–50). This underscores the pivotal role of dose information.

> Notably, DoseRank was evaluated on two screens; its behaviour in primary cells is untested; the ranking may change with other dose ranges. These controls motivate future work on richer evidence and pave the way for in silico screening.
